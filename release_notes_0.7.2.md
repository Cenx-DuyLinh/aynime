**Aynime 0.7.2** (includes 0.7.1)

**Pause on PlayTranslate capture**
- When you press PlayTranslate's capture button (X) while a video plays, Aynime pauses the video at the same moment. X still goes to PlayTranslate as before.
- Change the button or turn it off: ⋮ → Controller buttons → "Pause the video when PlayTranslate captures".

**Moved sites are followed automatically (from 0.7.1)**
- When a site you open from saved sites, My anime or Continue watching redirects to a new address, Aynime updates the saved site, My anime and Continue watching on its own. No question, no message.

Install: download `Aynime-0.7.2.apk` below on the Thor (or use ⋮ → Check for app update).
