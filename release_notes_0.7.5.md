## What's new

- **Subtitle band stays up**: while a subtitle track is loaded, the band no longer appears and disappears with each line. It stays on screen between lines, so the picture doesn't flicker and PlayTranslate's capture region always lines up.
- **Fixed two-line height**: the band is always exactly two lines tall at your text size. A long line that needs a third row shrinks the text to fit instead of growing the band. Changing the text size resizes the band to match. The "Keep the box up between lines" switch and the "Band height" slider are gone; darkness, colours, width, distance from bottom, soft sides and the lift when paused all work as before.
- **Security fixes**: a website could no longer reach Aynime's internal pages (home, search, error page). Try again on the error page only reopens normal web addresses, sites can't redirect into the internal pages, and the internal pages' bridge ignores messages from embedded frames.

Install: download `Aynime-0.7.5.apk` below, or use the in-app update.
