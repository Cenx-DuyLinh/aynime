## What's new

- **PlayTranslate reads the subtitle straight from Aynime**: when you capture over the subtitle band, PlayTranslate now takes the exact Japanese line from Aynime instead of reading the screen. Burned-in English showing through the band is never picked up and kanji are never misread, so you can set the box darkness to whatever looks good. Needs the PlayTranslate build with the Aynime exception; with any other PlayTranslate, captures work as before.
- **Shorter subtitle band**: the band still fits two lines but is much shorter, with tighter line spacing. The text now sits at the bottom of the band, where subtitles normally are, and a second line stacks above it.

Install: download `Aynime-0.7.6.apk` below, or use the in-app update.
